# Problem statement

Supply-chain operations teams need to identify at-risk shipments quickly, understand the
evidence behind the risk, connect active disruptions to affected shipments, and determine
whether fleet capacity is available to respond. These signals often live across separate
shipment, disruption, vehicle, and sensor records, which slows triage and makes decisions
difficult to trace.

SupplyGuard AI addresses this problem with a read-only control center for synthetic logistics
data. It is designed for demo and evaluation use and does not contain real customer or
production data.
